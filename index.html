About

hi, that’s me

i build circuits, write music and draw.

i live in the gap between hardware and things that act clever. i build circuits that notice stuff and react, teach models to cope with messy data, and simulate quantum things that make normal computers sweat.

my one trick: work out how the physics actually behaves, then write code that does the same. works on quantum sims, piles of logic gates and neural nets alike.

it isn’t all circuits though. i’ve been a musician for 10 years, and i write my own music too.

i draw on a tablet, because i can’t use a pen.

is there anything i’m not interested in at this point? i need to stop getting new interests.

The Bench
what’s on the desk right now
messy, on purpose 
 
in progress
Leaf Venation Net
a neural net that works out veins for a leaf shape i pick, evolving a fresh path from scratch every time. it exports drawings i can open in Adobe Fresco.
next: whole trees
 
lightening
Lightweight Fencing Grip
an italian grip in aluminium, additively manufactured. the design works, so now i’m taking material out with BESO in FreeCAD. aiming for around 60 g.
currently: solid, zero holes


testing
Automatic Headlights
a PICAXE-18M2 running assembler. one LDR reads how bright it is, a second one spots a car, and four LEDs do the rest.
tuning the torch threshold
