About

hi, that’s me

i build circuits, write music and draw.

i live in the gap between hardware and things that act clever. i build circuits that notice stuff and react, teach models to cope with messy data, and simulate quantum things that make normal computers sweat.

my one trick: work out how the physics actually behaves, then write code that does the same. works on quantum sims, piles of logic gates and neural nets alike.

it isn’t all circuits though. i’ve been a musician for 10 years, and i write my own music too.

i draw on a tablet, because i can’t use a pen.

is there anything i’m not interested in at this point? i need to stop getting new interests.

Failure Log

failed experiments, broken prototypes, abandoned projects and questionable decisions

filed under: learning (allegedly)
FL-001Automatic headlights, v1tuned wrong

what happened: the light sensor decided a torch was a car and midday was night. four LEDs, a lot of confidence, no accuracy at all.

lesson learned: the LDR has opinions and none of them are mine.

FL-002LED, no resistorcremated

what happened: pop. instantly. one LED, gone. a tiny smell of regret.

lesson learned: ohm’s law is not a suggestion. it’s a law. that’s in the name.

FL-003The pedal (80%)stuck at 80%

what happened: the last 20% is the part that makes it a pedal. it makes a noise. it is not a good noise.

lesson learned: 80% is not a percentage, it’s a lifestyle.

FL-004Half-built robot armabandoned

what happened: it twitched once. the twitch was the demo. i keep it on the shelf as a warning to other robots.

lesson learned: never power it on to ‘just see’.

FL-005The unfinished clocktechnically works

what happened: it tells the time now. the wrong one, confidently, which is arguably worse than not telling it.

lesson learned: a clock that’s wrong twice a day is better than a clock that’s wrong all day. mine is the second one.

FL-006Leaf venation net, early runproduced a spider

what happened: i asked for a leaf. the veins came out as a spider with a lot of ambition. it was beautiful. it was not a leaf.

lesson learned: tell the net what a leaf is before asking it to draw one.

FL-007Fencing grip, but make it solidworks. weighs a lot

what happened: the design works. it also weighs exactly what a solid grip weighs. the holes come later. the holes always come later.

lesson learned: you can’t BESO your way out of not having started.

FL-008Quantum chessonly an idea

what happened: i got as far as being very excited. that is the whole project so far. the excitement is excellent.

lesson learned: ideas are free. so is not finishing them.

FL-009Soldering iron, near the breadboardmodern art

what happened: melted. it’s a sculpture now. it’s staying on the desk because it looks intentional.

lesson learned: the breadboard is not a heatsink.

FL-010A website with a lamp that has opinionsongoing

what happened: the lamp complains. the lamp sulks. the lamp keeps a report on you. i regret nothing, which is the problem.

lesson learned: you’re reading it.
